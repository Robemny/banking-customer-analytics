# 🏦 Banking Customer Analytics

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes banking customer and marketing campaign data to identify patterns in customer behavior and explore factors associated with successful term deposit subscriptions.

Using Python and Pandas, I examine customer demographics, account characteristics, previous campaign interactions, and campaign outcomes to transform raw banking data into actionable business insights.

## Business Problem

Financial institutions use customer data to better understand engagement and improve the effectiveness of marketing campaigns. However, contacting customers without understanding which characteristics or behaviors are associated with successful outcomes can lead to inefficient outreach.

This analysis explores:

- Which customer characteristics are associated with successful subscriptions?
- How do account balance, age, occupation, and marital status relate to customer outcomes?
- How does previous campaign activity relate to subscription behavior?
- Where could customer segmentation help improve campaign targeting?

## Tools & Skills

**Python | Pandas | Data Cleaning | EDA | Data Profiling | Customer Analytics | Data Visualization**

## Dataset

This project uses a banking marketing dataset for educational and portfolio analysis. It does not contain M&T Bank customer information or proprietary employer data.
